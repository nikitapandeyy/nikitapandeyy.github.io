research

MetaRoBERTa: Gated Metadata Fusion for Fake News Detection

  • The idea. A learned gate decides, for each input, how much to trust the text and how much to trust its metadata.
  • The result. 90.5% Macro F1, 2.5 points above the baseline.
  • The check. One benchmark feature would not exist at prediction time. Without it, performance fell to 75.3%, so I report both numbers.
